C10 Evidence-Based Report Structure

Use this structure to write the final report using your webpage results and at least three reliable real-world sources.

Suggested headings:

Title: A clear title connected to the research context.
Introduction: Explain the real-world issue and cite reliable sources using APA in-text citations.
Research Question: State the question your hypothesis test answers.
Methodology: Define H₀, H₁, test direction, significance level, and data source.
Results: Report z statistic, p-value, decision, and graph interpretation.
Discussion: Explain what the result means and discuss Type I and Type II errors.
Conclusion: Give a contextual conclusion consistent with the statistical decision.
References: Include all sources in APA 7 format.
